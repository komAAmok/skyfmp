Put the master files from your own copy of Skyrim Special Edition here:

  Skyrim.esm
  Update.esm
  Dawnguard.esm
  HearthFires.esm
  Dragonborn.esm

They live in "<Skyrim Special Edition>\Data" and are not redistributable, which
is why the server archive ships without them. The load order in
server-settings.json must match what every connecting player has, in the same
order.

If you play with extra mods, add their .esp/.esm files here as well and list
them in "loadOrder" in server-settings.json in exactly the load order the
clients use.
